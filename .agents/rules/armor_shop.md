# Armor Shops

Armor shops are where the player can purchase armor to protect themselves from attack. Each armor shop is owned by a master armorsmith, who has a distinct specialty.
For example, one town may have an armor shop that only produces leather armor, while another might have one that produces chain mail.

Every day the master smith will create new sets of armor of varying strengh and quality. At a minimum the armor smith will produce one good quality armor, and one high quality
armor per day. The armor smith may once every week or so produce very high quality (i.e. strong) armor that also has magical effects.

Armor smiths have an apprentice who may also be selling low quality armor. These are practice pieces so many are produced per day.

## Quest Commissions
An armor shop may also commission quests for rare armor materials (e.g., harvesting monster hides from the forest).
* **Rewards:** Completing these quests rewards the player with a gold bounty and the exclusive opportunity to purchase the crafted item at a 20% discount.
