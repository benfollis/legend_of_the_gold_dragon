# Tavern and Inn

## The Bartender

All taverns have a bartender, whose name varies per town.
The bartender offers the following services

1. **Get a Room:** Costs **20 gold**. Safely logs out the character. Restores stamina at a rate of 12% per wall clock hour. If another player bribes the bartender for a key, the room door becomes unlocked, opening the player up to simulated offline PvP combat.
2. **Accept a Bribe (Key Bribe):** Costs **150 gold**. Yields the key to a random which may be a player's room who is currently checked into the inn or it may be an empty room. In the case of an occupied room, this Allows the buyer to enter and initiate simulated offline PvP combat (the defender is simulated based on their current gear, always wakes up to the door opening, and does not suffer a first-attack penalty).
3. **Buy a Drink:** Costs **10 gold**. Restores $+5$ stamina, but carries a 10% chance of intoxication (`inebriated` status) which halves net attack and defense stats for 10 minutes of wall clock time. Inebriation can be cured early using a detox potion from the Potion Shop.
4. **Solicit a Prostitute:** Costs **100 gold**. Consumes 1 stamina, instantly rewards $\text{Player Level} \times 75$ experience points, and has a 15% chance to restore $+15$ stamina.There is alos a 5% chance of contracting an STD. If the player alredy has an SDT, the prostitue will take all of the player's money, and the player will end up with 1 hit point.
