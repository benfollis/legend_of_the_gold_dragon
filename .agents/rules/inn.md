# Tavern and Inn

## The Bartender

All taverns have a bartender, whose name varies per town.
The bartender offers the following services

1. Get a room at the inn
2. Accept a bribe to provide keys to a room.
3. Buy a drink. A each drink increases stamina by 5, but gives a 10% chance to be intoxicated which halves attack and defense. The innebriated state lasts for 10 minutes wall clock time, or the user may drink a detox potion from the potion shop.
4. Solicit a prostitute. Sleeping with the prostitute takes 1 stamina but gains experience and has a 10% chance to restore 10 stamina.
