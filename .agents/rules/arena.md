# The Arena

## Fighting
At any point the player may chose to enter the arena and fight against gladiators.
If they win the fight they will receive a prize purse of 10000 gold * the level of the gladiator they just fought, and be completely healed.
Upon wining the crowd may ask them to kill their opponent at 10% probability. If they do so, they will forefeit their prize to the owner of the arenam but the crowd will be pleased. If they do NOT the crowd will be angered.
The player may stop fighting upon wining a fight and exit the arena.
If they lose the fight (i.e. damage exceeds their remaining HP) one of two things will hapen
If the crowd is angered, then with 90% probability the crowd will demand the opponent jill the player and the player will suffer the death penalty as described in player rules.
If the crowd is pleased, then the opponent will always spare the player and they will be left with one hp, and be unable to enter any more fights for the day.

The players current level +- 2 (Randomly chosen) is the level of the first opponent

If the crowd is neither angered nor pleased then the opponent will spare the player at 90% probability and otherwise kill the player.

A player winning the fight may chose to fight the next gladiator, which will be 1 level higher than their current opponent.

## Betting
The player may place bets on the victor of a fight, provided they are not a participant. Fights are running every mintue of wall clock time. The participant naems and odds / levels re randomly generated, however the opponents should be +1 2 to each other.
The "odds' for the fight are set randomly between 1:1 and 1:3
