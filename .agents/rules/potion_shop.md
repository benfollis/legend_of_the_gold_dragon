# Potion Shops

Potion shops are where players can buy consumable items to heal, restore stamina, or cure status effects. Potions can be used in town or during forest exploration to maintain player stamina and health.

## Potion Inventory
The shop carries a constant inventory of the following standard consumables:

| Potion Name | Purchase Cost (Gold) | Effects & Mechanics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Detox Potion** | 50 | Instantly removes the `inebriated` (intoxicated) status effect, restoring Attack and Defense to normal. |
| **Health Potion** | 150 | Instantly restores **+100 Hit Points** (up to the player's max HP). Can be used during combat or in town. |
| **Stamina Elixir** | 300 | Instantly restores **+50 Stamina** (up to the player's max Stamina). Allows the player to continue exploring without fatigue. |
