# Bank Branches

All civilized towns host a branch of the Royal Bank, offering secure gold storage and passive growth.

## Banking Rules
* **Security:** Gold stored in the bank is completely safe from the death penalty. Storing gold here is the primary method of protecting wealth.
* **Transactions:** Deposits and withdrawals are completely free with unlimited transactions.
* **Daily Interest:** Balance in the bank accumulates at a rate of **1% interest per daily reset**.
* **Interest Cap:** To prevent runaway compound interest and ensure economic balance, interest gains are capped at a maximum of **1,000 gold earned per day**.
