The town is ruled over by the mayor who is a subbordinate of the golden dragon. The mayor is protected by the town guard, who are all elite level 20 soldiers with mid level equipment. A town guard typically has between 8 and 20 guardsmen.  If you attack the mayors mansion, andefeat all of the town guard, you may fight the mayor who is a young golden dragon in disguise. Defeating the mayor presents you the option to become the mayor of that town.
