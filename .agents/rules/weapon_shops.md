# Weapon Shops

Weapon shops work identically to armor shops, except they produce **weapons** instead of armor to enhance the player's Attack stat. Each shop is owned by a master weaponsmith who rotates through different daily designs.

Like armor shops, the master smith will create at least one good quality weapon and one high quality weapon per day. The apprentice sells cheap, low-quality practice weapons. Magical, very high-quality weapons are produced once every week or so.

## Quest Commissions
A weapon shop may commission quests for rare forging materials (e.g., hunting specialized beast ores in the deep forest).
* **Rewards:** Fulfilling the commission grants a gold reward and opens up a 20% discount on the newly forged item.
