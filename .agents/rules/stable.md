# Stables

Stables are available in all major towns and allow players to purchase a horse for outright ownership.

## Horse Ownership Rules
* **Purchase Cost:** Outright ownership of a horse costs **2,000 gold**. Once purchased, the horse belongs to the player permanently (no deposits or daily fees).
* **Stamina Discount:** While riding their horse, the stamina cost of traveling along civilized roads and forest paths is **reduced to 1/10th of the normal cost** (i.e., **0.1 stamina per kilometer** instead of 1.0).
* **Deep Forest Caveat:** Horses cannot be ridden off-path during Deep Forest exploration. If a player steps off the path, the horse is safely tied to a signpost at the path's edge and remains there until the player returns.
