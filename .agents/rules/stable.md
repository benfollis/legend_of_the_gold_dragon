# Stables

Stables are available in all major towns and allow players to rent horses to expedite travel between towns.

## Horse Rental Rules
* **Deposit:** Renting a horse requires a fully refundable deposit of **500 gold**. The deposit is returned to the player upon returning the horse to any stable.
* **Daily Fee:** Renting a horse costs a continuous fee of **50 gold per daily reset**. If the player lacks the gold to cover the daily fee during reset, the stable master reclaims the horse.
* **Stamina Discount:** While riding a rented horse, the stamina cost of traveling along civilized roads and forest paths is **reduced by 50%** (i.e., **0.5 stamina per kilometer** instead of 1.0).
* **Deep Forest Caveat:** Horses cannot be ridden off-path during Deep Forest exploration. If a player steps off the path, the horse is safely tied to a signpost at the path's edge and remains there until the player returns.
