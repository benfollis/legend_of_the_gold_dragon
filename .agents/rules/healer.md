# Healer's Shops

Healer's shops are run by local priests and herbalists who can instantly mend a player's injuries.

## Healing Services
* **Service Fee:** Full recovery costs **2 gold per missing Hit Point**.
* **Effect:** Instantly restores the player's current Hit Points to their maximum capacity.
* **Limitations:** The healer cannot restore stamina or cure `inebriated` status (for which players must sleep or purchase potions, respectively). The healer can only mend living players—if a player dies in combat, they must undergo standard resurrection (which resets them to their last town square after lockout).
